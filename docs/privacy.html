SeatTime — Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 6, 2026

SeatTime ("the App") is developed by Dan Johnson ("we", "us", "our"). This policy explains what data the App collects, how it is used, and your rights.

1. Data We Collect

Data you provide:

  • Teen driver's first name (stored locally and, if you choose to sync, in Firebase)
  • US state selection for permit hour requirements
  • Drive log entries including date, duration, time of day, weather, and road conditions
  • Email address (only if you create an account to enable multi-parent sharing)

Data collected automatically during drives:

  • GPS location (used solely to record the driving route during an active session — location is never tracked when the App is not in use)
  • Sunrise and sunset times (derived from location to determine day/night driving)

2. How We Use Your Data

  • To track and display driving practice hours toward your state's permit requirements
  • To generate PDF and JSON exports of driving logs
  • To enable multi-parent sharing via invite codes (requires an account)
  • To calculate progress toward milestones

We do not use your data for advertising, analytics, profiling, or any purpose beyond the App's core functionality.

3. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore) — used only if you create an account for multi-parent sharing. Firebase's privacy policy: firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Expo / EAS (build and update infrastructure) — expo.dev/privacy

We do not use any advertising SDKs, analytics platforms, or AI services.

4. Data Storage and Security

Drive logs are stored locally on your device by default. If you create an account, data is synced to Google Firebase Cloud Firestore servers. All data in transit is encrypted via HTTPS/TLS.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with any third party. Drive data is shared only between parents/guardians who join the same family group via an invite code within the App.

6. Your Rights

You may at any time:

  • Export all your data as PDF or JSON from the Settings screen
  • Delete your account and all associated cloud data from the Settings screen
  • Use the App without creating an account (all data stays on-device only)

Under UK GDPR and applicable US state privacy laws, you have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data. Contact us to exercise these rights.

7. Children's Privacy

SeatTime is designed to be used by parents and guardians to log a teen's supervised driving practice. The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The parent or guardian manages the App and all data within it.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the most recent revision.
